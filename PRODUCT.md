## Stack

## Users

Primary: hiring managers and IT leads evaluating Mark Jason B. Ramos for IT, systems or infrastructure roles, usually scanning quickly on a laptop or phone between other candidates. Web and SEO clients are a secondary audience.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that earns an interview. Success means a hiring manager believes Mark can be trusted with the systems a business depends on, then downloads the CV or emails him.

The visitor should remember how he thinks about systems: how they connect, fail and get fixed.
